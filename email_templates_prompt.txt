
Transactional Email Requirements ;

Define email templates for:

- Account verification if required
- Password reset
- Order confirmation
- Payment failed
- Order dispatched
- Tracking information
- Order cancelled
- Refund issued
- Return or exchange update
- Back-in-stock notification
- Contact form acknowledgement
- Newsletter subscription confirmation if double opt-in is used

Answer:

Below are concise transactional email templates with reusable placeholders.

Common placeholders: {{customer_name}}, {{store_name}}, {{order_number}}, {{support_email}}, {{cta_url}}, {{tracking_number}}, {{carrier}}, {{refund_amount}}.

Account Verification

Trigger: Sent when account verification is required.

Subject: Verify your {{store_name}} account
Preheader: Confirm your email address to finish setting up your account.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Please verify your email address to finish creating your {{store_name}} account.

CTA: Verify account

This link will expire in {{expiry_time}}.

If you did not create this account, you can ignore this email.

Password Reset

Trigger: User requests a password reset.

Subject: Reset your {{store_name}} password
Preheader: Use this secure link to choose a new password.

Hi {{customer_name}},

We received a request to reset the password for your {{store_name}} account.

CTA: Reset password

This link will expire in {{expiry_time}}. If you did not request this, no action is needed.

Order Confirmation

Trigger: Order successfully placed.

Subject: Order confirmed: #{{order_number}}
Preheader: We've received your order and are preparing it now.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Thanks for your order. We've received order #{{order_number}} and will notify you when it ships.

Order total: {{order_total}}
Shipping to: {{shipping_address}}

CTA: View order

Payment Failed

Trigger: Payment attempt fails.

Subject: Payment failed for order #{{order_number}}
Preheader: Please update your payment method to complete your order.

Hi {{customer_name}},

We were unable to process payment for order #{{order_number}}.

Please update your payment details so we can continue processing your order.

CTA: Update payment

If payment is not completed by {{payment_deadline}}, the order may be cancelled.

Order Dispatched

Trigger: Order leaves warehouse or is handed to carrier.

Subject: Your order has been dispatched
Preheader: Order #{{order_number}} is on its way.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Good news. Order #{{order_number}} has been dispatched.

Carrier: {{carrier}}
Estimated delivery: {{estimated_delivery_date}}

CTA: View order status

Tracking Information

Trigger: Tracking number becomes available or updates.

Subject: Tracking details for order #{{order_number}}
Preheader: Track your delivery with the link inside.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Tracking is now available for order #{{order_number}}.

Carrier: {{carrier}}
Tracking number: {{tracking_number}}

CTA: Track package

Order Cancelled

Trigger: Order is cancelled by customer, admin, payment timeout, or inventory issue.

Subject: Order #{{order_number}} has been cancelled
Preheader: Your order cancellation has been processed.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Order #{{order_number}} has been cancelled.

Reason: {{cancellation_reason}}

If you were charged, any eligible refund will be processed according to your payment provider's timeline.

CTA: View order

Refund Issued

Trigger: Refund is successfully initiated.

Subject: Refund issued for order #{{order_number}}
Preheader: Your refund has been processed.

Hi {{customer_name}},

A refund of {{refund_amount}} has been issued for order #{{order_number}}.

Refunds usually appear on your original payment method within {{refund_timeline}}.

CTA: View refund details

Return Or Exchange Update

Trigger: Return/exchange requested, approved, received, rejected, or completed.

Subject: Update on your return for order #{{order_number}}
Preheader: Here's the latest status of your return or exchange.

Hi {{customer_name}},

There is an update on your return or exchange for order #{{order_number}}.

Status: {{return_status}}
Details: {{return_update_message}}

CTA: View return status

Back-In-Stock Notification

Trigger: Product becomes available after user requested notification.

Subject: {{product_name}} is back in stock
Preheader: The item you wanted is available again.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Good news. {{product_name}} is back in stock.

Availability may be limited.

CTA: Shop now

Contact Form Acknowledgement

Trigger: User submits contact form.

Subject: We received your message
Preheader: Our team will get back to you soon.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Thanks for contacting {{store_name}}. We've received your message and our team will respond as soon as possible.

Reference: {{ticket_reference}}

Message topic: {{contact_subject}}

Newsletter Double Opt-In

Trigger: User subscribes to newsletter when double opt-in is enabled.

Subject: Confirm your newsletter subscription
Preheader: One more step to start receiving updates.

Hi {{customer_name}},

Please confirm that you want to receive emails from {{store_name}}.

CTA: Confirm subscription

If you did not request this, you can ignore this email.

Transactional emails should not include marketing content unless the customer has separately opted in to marketing.
